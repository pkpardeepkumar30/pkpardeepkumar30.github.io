My engineering background shapes the way I build: understand the problem, make the important decisions explicit, and validate the result. I have applied that approach across energy, semiconductors, engineering simulation, finance, and scientific computing.

## Scientific computing and research

At CWI Amsterdam and TU Delft, I developed numerical methods and software for modelling complex fluid transport. The work connected mathematical formulation, robust implementation, and careful validation, including a reformulated thermodynamic optimisation algorithm that achieved an approximately 30× speedup over a traditional nested formulation.

My doctoral thesis has been submitted. The underlying methods and results are documented in my [research overview](../research/) and [publications](../publications/).

## Industrial software

At Shell, I worked on performance-intensive simulation and data workflows. A representative GPU-enabled workflow went from approximately 30 minutes to 15 seconds, while a multi-client data workflow achieved more than 15× higher throughput.

At ASM International, I developed simulator features for semiconductor equipment and improved the structure and maintainability of existing software. Earlier work at KLA-Tencor, Altair, Fluidyn, and Aakraya Research spanned simulation engines, engineering tools, parallel solvers, and financial-data systems.

## Independent product building
